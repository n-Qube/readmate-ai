# ReadMate AI WebMCP demo recording runbook

This is the one-pass recording script for the OpenAI WebMCP Challenge. The finished public YouTube video must remain under three minutes. Target **2:40–2:50** after trimming; never speed up or replace the Twi playback in a way that misrepresents the live product.

## Stop conditions

Do not record or publish the final take until all of these are true in a fresh browser session:

- `https://app.readmate.n-qube.com/challenge` loads with valid HTTPS;
- the dedicated judge account signs in without requiring the entrant's inbox, phone, payment, or recovery action;
- exactly six ReadMate tools are discoverable on the approved signed-in workspace and none are exposed on the public landing or blocked account/billing routes;
- the judge library contains one copy of **Reading and digital learning in Ghana**;
- live Asante Twi playback is clear, audible, and generated by the deployed backend;
- the challenge page and challenge feed are absent from the judge library before the write demonstrations;
- notifications, bookmarks, unrelated tabs, personal email, tokens, account identifiers, and developer consoles are out of frame.

If any live tool, sign-in, form, or Twi playback step fails, stop the take and fix the product. Do not splice in a local-only success as if it came from the submitted build.

## Capture setup

- Record 1920×1080 at 30 fps with system audio and a clean microphone track.
- Use browser zoom between 110% and 125% so prompts, tool results, disclosures, and buttons remain readable.
- Enable Do Not Disturb and close all unrelated applications and tabs.
- Begin already signed in to the dedicated judge account; the video does not need to expose credentials.
- Use spoken narration plus captured ReadMate/Twi audio only. Do not add background music.
- Keep the Twi output at its natural speed and loud enough to understand without clipping.

## Edit and publication checks

- Export one continuous 1080p video under 3:00; target 2:48 and verify the encoded duration.
- Listen once on headphones and once through laptop or phone speakers. Narration and Twi must both be intelligible.
- Confirm every product result shown came from the final submitted domain and deployed backend.
- Remove dead air only; do not hide failed actions, warning states, or manual confirmations.
- Use a ReadMate-owned title card or interface still. Avoid third-party artwork and unlicensed music.
- Upload to YouTube with public visibility and verify playback while signed out before adding the link to Devpost.
- Freeze the final video and submitted build after the deadline unless the organizers approve a correction.
